# Promotion Document

Promotion is about showing consistent impact **at the next level**. Assemble
evidence mapped to the rubric, get it in front of decision-makers early, and make
others' jobs easy.

## When to use

- Preparing a promotion case or self-review.
- Maintaining a brag document of accomplishments.
- Asking for a raise or level change.
- Trigger phrases: "promotion doc", "promotion case", "brag document", "get
  promoted", "level expectations", "performance review", "ask for a raise".

## Instructions

1. Get the **rubric** for the next level; map evidence to each dimension.
2. Lead with **impact and outcomes**, not activities or hours.
3. Show consistency over time and growing scope (breadth + ambiguity handled).
4. Include **leverage**: what you enabled in others (mentoring, unblocking, standards).
5. Quantify: metrics, revenue, latency, incidents prevented, time saved.
6. Gather **peer and cross-team testimonials** early.
7. Share the doc with your manager well before the cycle; align on gaps and a plan.

### Evidence-mapped structure

```markdown
# Promotion case: <name> -> <next level>
## Summary
One paragraph: sustained impact at the next level across <dimensions>.

## Impact (mapped to rubric)
### Technical excellence
- Redesigned checkout: p95 2.5s->1.0s, +4% conversion (Q1).
- Authored ADR adopting Redis; adopted by 3 teams.
### Ownership & scope
- Led migration of payments to new provider with zero downtime.
### Leverage / people
- Mentored 2 juniors; one promoted. Ran the weekly design review.

## Testimonials
> "..." — peer, team

## Gaps & plan
- Gap: cross-org influence -> plan: lead a cross-team RFC next quarter.
```

### Brag document (keep year-round)

```markdown
## Wins
- [date] shipped X — impact
## Feedback
- [date] "…"  (who)
## Metrics I moved
- …
## Skills I grew
- …
```

## Examples

### Good — outcome + leverage + evidence

> "Drove Redis adoption (ADR 0007) now used by 3 teams; cut feed p95 1.2s→40ms;
> mentored 2 engineers, one promoted. Maps to 'technical excellence' and
> 'leverage' at L5."

### Bad — activity list with no outcomes

> "Attended 40 meetings, wrote 200 commits, fixed many bugs." (No impact, no
> scope, no mapping to level.)

## Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why it hurts | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Activity/hours instead of impact | Doesn't justify a level | Outcomes + metrics |
| No mapping to rubric | Committee can't judge | Evidence per dimension |
| Waiting until review time | Rushed, no alignment | Maintain a brag doc |
| Only solo work | Misses leadership signal | Show leverage/mentoring |
| No testimonials | Missing corroboration | Gather peers early |
| Surprising your manager | No advocacy | Align months ahead |
| Ignoring gaps | Weakens the case | Name gaps + growth plan |
