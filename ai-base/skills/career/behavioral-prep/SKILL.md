# Behavioral Interview Prep

Behavioral questions predict future behavior from past behavior. Prepare a
**story bank** from your real experience and deliver it with STAR — concise and
outcome-focused.

## When to use

- Preparing for behavioral interviews.
- Answering "tell me about a time when…" questions.
- Writing resume bullets or promotion narratives from past work.
- Trigger phrases: "behavioral interview", "STAR method", "tell me about a
  time", "interview stories", "conflict question", "strengths and weaknesses".

## Instructions

1. Build a **story bank** of 6–10 real stories spanning themes (below).
2. Format each with **STAR**: Situation, Task, Action, Result — plus a reflection.
3. Emphasize **your** actions ("I decided…"), not just the team's.
4. Quantify results where possible; always land a clear outcome/learning.
5. Keep answers ~2 minutes; stop and let them probe.
6. Map each story to multiple competencies so you can reuse it.
7. Have a growth story and a genuine weakness + how you addressed it.

### Themes to cover

```text
Impact · Leadership · Conflict · Failure/mistake · Ambiguity · Ownership
Collaboration · Disagreement with a manager · Pushing back on scope
Learning fast · Mentoring · Prioritization · Cross-team communication
```

### STAR template

```markdown
## Story: cut checkout latency 60%
- Situation: p95 was 2.5s; conversion dropping.
- Task: I owned improving checkout performance.
- Action: profiled, found N+1 queries, added indexes + Redis read-through,
  shipped behind a flag.
- Result: p95 1.0s; conversion +4%; became the team's caching pattern.
- Reflection: measure before optimizing; small infra wins compound.
```

## Examples

### Good — specific, personal, quantified

> "Conflict: a teammate and I disagreed on the data model. I proposed we write a
> short ADR comparing options; we aligned on the hybrid. Shipped two weeks later
> with no rework."

### Bad — generic and passive

> "We had a conflict and things worked out." (No specifics, no actions, no result,
> no learning.)

## Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why it hurts | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Vague/generic answers | Unconvincing | Real, specific STAR stories |
| Saying "we" only | Can't see your impact | Own your actions |
| No result/learning | Incomplete story | Quantify outcome + reflect |
| Rambling (5+ min) | Loses the room | ~2 min, then pause |
| Trashing former teammates | Red flag | Focus on actions/system |
| Weakness = "I work too hard" | Cliché, evasive | Real weakness + growth |
| One story for everything | Repetitive | Story bank by theme |
