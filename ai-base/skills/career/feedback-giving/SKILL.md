# Giving & Receiving Feedback

Feedback is a gift only if it's specific, timely, and about behavior, not the
person. Aim to be both **caring and direct** (radical candor).

## When to use

- Delivering constructive or positive feedback.
- Writing peer/upward feedback (reviews, 360s).
- Receiving feedback and responding without defensiveness.
- Trigger phrases: "give feedback", "constructive criticism", "peer feedback",
  "SBI", "radical candor", "how to tell someone", "receive feedback".

## Instructions

1. Be **specific and behavioral**, not about character ("the PR had no tests", not "you're careless").
2. Use **SBI**: Situation → Behavior → Impact; then invite their view.
3. Give it **timely** and privately for criticism; praise publicly.
4. Pair caring with directness — vague feedback helps no one.
5. Make it actionable: what could change next time?
6. When receiving: listen, ask questions, thank them, reflect — don't defend instantly.
7. Separate **observation** from **judgment**; describe what you saw.

### SBI template

```text
Situation: In yesterday's review meeting,
Behavior:  you interrupted Sam three times mid-sentence,
Impact:    Sam stopped sharing ideas and the discussion narrowed.
Ask:       what happened from your side? Next time could we let each person finish?
```

### Radical candor quadrants

```text
                  Care Personally
                  low        high
Challenge  high   Obnoxious  Radical Candor ✓
Personally low    Manipulative  Ruinous Empathy
```

### Receiving feedback

```text
1. Listen fully; don't interrupt or plan your rebuttal.
2. Ask for specifics/examples.
3. Thank them for the courage to say it.
4. Reflect; decide what to act on; follow up.
```

## Examples

### Good — specific, behavioral, actionable

> "The migration PR merged without a rollback plan (situation+behavior). During
> staging it took hours to recover (impact). Let's add a rollback section to the
> template."

### Bad — vague or personal

> "You need to be more proactive." (No situation, behavior, or impact; feels like
> a character attack.)

## Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why it hurts | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Personality/character attacks | Triggers defensiveness | Describe behavior |
| Vague feedback ("do better") | Unactionable | SBI + specifics |
| Only negative, saved for review | Blindside, unfair | Timely, continuous |
| Public criticism | Humiliates | Private; praise publicly |
| Ruinous empathy (avoid the truth) | No growth | Care **and** challenge |
| Sandwich method | Confusing, dilutes message | Direct, kind, separate |
| Rejecting feedback instantly | Stops learning | Listen, ask, reflect |
