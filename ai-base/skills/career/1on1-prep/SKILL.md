# 1:1 Preparation

A 1:1 is for the report's agenda, not status (that's standup). Use it for growth,
alignment, blockers, and feedback — and follow up on action items.

## When to use

- Preparing for a one-on-one (as report or manager).
- Wanting to raise growth, concerns, or feedback.
- Structuring a recurring 1:1 or documenting outcomes.
- Trigger phrases: "1:1", "one-on-one", "meeting with my manager", "agenda",
  "raise a concern", "career conversation", "check-in".

## Instructions

1. Come with an agenda; lead with what matters most to you.
2. Share context (wins, blockers, risks) briefly — not a status recital.
3. Bring **topics**, not just problems: propose options and ask for input.
4. Make growth concrete: skills, visibility, next-role expectations, timelines.
5. Give and ask for feedback with specifics.
6. End by confirming action items, owners, and dates.
7. Keep a running doc; review last time's follow-ups first.

### Agenda template

```markdown
## 1:1 — <date>
### Follow-ups from last time
- [ ] …
### Wins / context (2 min)
- …
### Topics (what I want from you)
- Growth: I want to lead a design review — how can I get there by Q3?
- Blocker: I'm stuck on <X>; I'd like <help/decision>.
### Feedback (both ways)
- To you: …
- For me: …
### Actions
- [ ] <owner> — <what> — <when>
```

### Raising a concern (SBI)

```text
Situation: In yesterday's planning,
Behavior:  the scope doubled without re-estimating,
Impact:    the team committed to a date we can't hit.
Ask:       can we re-scope before Friday?
```

## Examples

### Good — agenda, ask, follow-up

> "Topic: I'd like to present at the next architecture review to build visibility.
> Blocker: the payments spike needs a decision on the provider by Wed. Action: I'll
> draft the options doc; you weigh in Thursday."

### Bad — status dump, no agenda

> Reciting tickets for 25 minutes; no growth, feedback, or asks; nothing changes
> afterward.

## Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why it hurts | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Status update meeting | Wastes the 1:1 | Use standup/docs for status |
| No agenda | Drifts, misses priorities | Bring a short agenda |
| Problems with no options | Puts all on manager | Propose options + ask |
| Vague growth talk | Nothing progresses | Concrete skills/visibility/timeline |
| No follow-ups | Repeated topics, no progress | Track actions in a doc |
| Avoiding hard topics | Resentment builds | Raise with SBI, calmly |
