# UX Research & Synthesis

A consolidated research skill. Each method has a detailed reference in
`references/`: `ux-research-methods.md`, `ux-personas.md`, `empathy-mapping.md`,
`journey-mapping.md`, `ux-storyboard.md`, `double-diamond.md`,
`like-wish-what-if.md`, `feature-prioritization.md`, `low-effort-high-reward.md`.

## When to use

- Deciding what research to do and when.
- Turning notes/interviews into personas, empathy maps, or journey maps.
- Framing a design problem (Double Diamond).
- Running a critique or retrospective.
- Prioritizing a backlog or audit findings (impact vs effort).
- Trigger phrases: "user research", "personas", "journey map", "empathy map",
  "storyboard", "design process", "prioritize", "quick wins", "where do I start".

## Instructions

1. Clarify the question first (behavior vs attitude; qualitative vs quantitative) before picking a method.
2. Match the method to the product stage: discover → define → develop → deliver.
3. Synthesize raw data into artifacts (personas, empathy/journey maps) with evidence quotes.
4. Ground personas in real research; avoid invented demographics.
5. Use journey maps to expose pain points across time, not a single screen.
6. Prioritize recommendations by impact vs effort and present a clear first step.

### Method selection

| Question | Method |
|---|---|
| Why do users behave this way? | Interviews, contextual inquiry |
| How well does this flow work? | Usability testing |
| How many / how often? | Surveys, analytics, A/B tests |
| What do users need early on? | Discovery interviews, diary studies |

### Synthesis artifacts

- **Personas** — goals, behaviors, frustrations, backed by quotes.
- **Empathy map** — Says / Thinks / Does / Feels.
- **Journey map** — stages × actions/emotions/pain points/opportunities.
- **Storyboard** — panel-by-panel scenario.

### Prioritization (impact × effort)

```
Quick wins      high reward / low effort   -> do first
Big bets        high reward / high effort  -> plan
Fill-ins        low reward / low effort    -> maybe
Time sinks      low reward / high effort   -> drop
```

## Examples

### Good — method matched to stage, evidence-backed

> Stage: post-launch retention. Method: 8 customer interviews + funnel
> analytics. Output: 3 personas, a journey map with 5 drop-off points, ranked
> by impact/effort. First step: fix onboarding step 2 (low effort, high reward).

### Bad — method mismatch, invented data

> We'll persona-fy "Sarah, 27, likes coffee" and guess the pain points.

## Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why it hurts | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Leading with a method | Wrong tool for the question | Start from the question |
| Personas without research | Fiction misleads the team | Ground in evidence |
| Journey map = one screen | Misses end-to-end friction | Map across stages/time |
| Prioritizing by opinion | Builds the wrong things | Score impact vs effort |
| Research with no decision | Wasted effort | Tie findings to a next step |
